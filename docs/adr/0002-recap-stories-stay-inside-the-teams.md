# Recap stories stay inside the two teams

The family live page is shared at a public link, but Recap stories are readable only by team members of the two teams that played. GameChanger publishes its recap stories more openly. We chose not to because a story is the most quotable, most searchable thing Sandlot writes about a 9-year-old, and once a story is public it can't be taken back.

## Consequences

- A share image that leaves the app names a child only when the person sharing has claimed that child. Everyone else shares a team-level image (score and team headline).
- Anyone holding only the public link sees the score and linescore, but no Recap teaser and no story.
