# Design

The app's screens are designed in Figma: [Sandlot](https://www.figma.com/design/J8F4zRkkV63ji3AYhcHHwA). The **Viewer** page holds the final screens and the clickable prototype, **Components** holds the library, **Stress test** holds worst-case layouts, and **Archive** holds past explorations.

Each note below records the decisions behind one design task and the Figma node IDs of its screens. The words they use are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md).

In the order they were designed:

1. [Video tab](spec-update-video-tab.md)
2. [Game list](spec-update-game-list.md)
3. [Game states](spec-update-game-states.md): before the first pitch, rain delay, and Final with a summary
4. [Recap story](spec-update-recap-story.md)
5. [Brand](spec-update-brand.md): the logo and how it lines up with the wordmark
6. [Fix a play and Delay](spec-update-fix-and-delay.md)
7. [Time limit, called and suspended games](spec-update-time-limit-called-suspended.md)
8. [Prototype wiring](spec-update-prototype.md)

Sample data in the designs follows Mustang rules for one game, Hawks 2 at Marlins 1 in the Bottom 5. The names in it are made up.
